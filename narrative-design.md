# Narrative Design — Späti Simulator (working title)

> Built with **John Truby's *The Anatomy of Story*** per the factory rule
> (`factory-reference/knowledge/playbooks/narrative-and-story.md`). The **main arc** uses Truby's **22 steps**;
> **every side story** uses Truby's **7 key steps**. Goal: *one gripping, dramatic main story* +
> *many curiosity side stories that bring Berlin and the Späti to life* — its diversity, quirks, history.

---

## 1. Premise & designing principle

**Premise (one sentence):** A rootless twenty-something inherits his late uncle's struggling Berlin
Späti and, over one year, must decide whether the place — and the Kiez — is a wallet to cash out or a
home worth standing for.

**Designing principle** *(story process + original execution)*: **the four seasons of one year, told
from behind one counter.** The growth of a person and a neighbourhood is shown through the turning of
the year — Summer's ease → Autumn's pressure → Winter's crisis → a Spring coda — where *every stranger
who comes through the door at night is a facet of the city*, and the hero's change is measured by how he
treats them as the cold closes in. *(This is Truby's own seasonal designing-principle pattern; the
night/season cycle that drives the management loop **is** the dramatic structure.)*

**Theme / moral argument:** **A place is its people; you belong somewhere by taking responsibility for
it.** The opposing value the story argues against: *that a place (and a person) is just an asset to be
priced, optimized, and sold.*

---

## 2. The hero — `W × A = C`, ghost, needs, desire

- **Hero:** *You* — mid-20s, came back to Berlin after years away (a drift, a failure elsewhere), now
  owner of the Späti you grew up in, left to you by **Onkel Cem**, who ran it for thirty years and
  raised you in this Kiez.
- **Ghost (the past wound):** you left the Kiez to "become something," fell out with Cem over it, and
  he died before you made peace. You carry guilt and rootlessness — and a resentment of Cem's
  "stubborn" refusal to ever leave or sell.
- **Weakness — psychological:** rootless, non-committal; treats the Späti as temporary, keeps one foot
  out the door; afraid to belong (afraid of becoming "stuck," as you judged Cem to be).
- **Weakness — moral** *(you are hurting others)*: you see people transactionally — customers as
  wallets, the Kiez as a market. You'll gouge, cut corners, take the sketchy supplier, undercut a
  neighbour, and you're tempted to flip the place and run.
- **Psychological need:** to put down roots — to commit and belong.
- **Moral need:** to learn the Späti and the Kiez are a web of people you're responsible *to*, and to
  act with care rather than calculation.
- **Basic action (A):** *run the Späti through one year* (the management loop is the crucible).
- **Desire (starts low, intensifies — never swapped):** *keep the lights on this month* → *make it
  thrive* → *save the Späti and the Kiez from being erased.* One spine, rising stakes.
- **Change (C):** from a man cashing out a building to a man who has chosen a home and a people.

---

## 3. Character web (four-corner opposition)

- **Hero** — the inheritor (above).
- **Main opponent** *(competes for the SAME goal — the future of the building/the corner — and attacks
  the hero's weakness)*: the **Lindner Group**, a property developer converting the Kiez to luxury
  flats and a chain "Spätkauf," fronted by its agent **Frau Brandt**. Brandt attacks the hero's exact
  weakness: she offers him the **easy money and escape he secretly wants** — sell, walk away, be free.
  She is *humanized, not cartoonish*: she grew up in a Kiez that was erased and tells herself this is
  just how cities breathe.
- **Ally:** **Mehmet**, the döner-shop neighbour and Cem's oldest friend — the conscience/mentor — and,
  collectively, **the regulars** (the Kiez itself).
- **Fake-ally opponent** *(seems to help, actually undermines the true goal)*: **Tobias**, the
  big-tipping startup transplant who "loves the authentic Kiez" — warm, generous, lonely, genuinely
  likes the hero — and is the leading edge of exactly the money that is erasing the place.
- **Fake-opponent ally** *(late turn)*: **Frau Brandt** — pure opponent until a third-act turn where she
  gives the hero the one warning/lever that makes the stand possible, complicating the morality.

## 4. The main arc — Truby's 22 steps, mapped to the four seasons

*(Beats are **delivered in play** as scripted vignettes / events / decision-points fired at the noted
season + flag/reputation triggers. The management loop continues underneath throughout.)*

**— SUMMER (Act I: ease, the inheritance) —**
1. **Self-revelation, need & desire (frame):** we aim at the hero choosing the Kiez over the cash and
   reconciling with Cem. *(Authoring target, not shown yet.)*
2. **Ghost & story world:** open on Cem's wake spilling out of the Späti; you arrive to the shuttered
   shop, the keys in your hand, the Kiez warm and humming. *(Opening cinematic beat + first night.)*
3. **Weakness & need:** the first nights — you serve people as transactions, eye the place as something
   to flip; Mehmet watches. *(Early vignettes establish the transactional default.)*
4. **Inciting event:** Frau Brandt arrives with a **buyout offer + a rent-hike notice** — generous,
   tempting, with a deadline. *(Act-I decision-point beat.)*
5. **Desire (low):** just cover this month's rent and keep the doors open. *(The management goal goes live.)*
6. **Ally/allies:** Mehmet and the regulars rally around the new owner; the corner adopts you.
7. **Opponent & mystery:** the Lindner Group / Brandt revealed as buying up the block; the **mystery —
   why did Cem, broke as he was, never sell?**
8. **Fake-ally opponent:** **Tobias** befriends you — big tips, big ideas, "you should make this place a
   destination" — pulling you toward the very thing that prices the Kiez out.

**— AUTUMN (Act II: pressure, the drive) —**
9. **First revelation & decision (changed motive):** you learn the **building is nearly bought out
   around you**; you decide to actually *fight to keep it* (desire intensifies past mere survival).
10. **Plan:** make the Späti **indispensable** — the Kiez's hub (events, the bench, credit for regulars,
    the bottle-collector welcomed) — and quietly organize the remaining tenants.
11. **Opponent's plan & counterattack:** the chain **"Spätkauf" opens down the block**; rent jumps; the
    buyout is sweetened. Foot traffic bleeds.
12. **Drive:** the Autumn grind — and the temptation to **win dirty**: gouge tourists, take the sketchy
    supplier, undercut Mehmet. *(Delivered through the management choices + Integrity flags.)*
13. **Attack by ally:** **Mehmet calls you out** — you're becoming the thing you're fighting. The moral
    mirror. *(A hard vignette gated on accumulated Integrity-negative choices.)*

**— WINTER (Act III: crisis) —**
14. **Apparent defeat:** the cold lands a blow — a beloved regular is **evicted / leaves / passes**, the
    till can't make rent, and **Cem's hidden debt** surfaces. The Späti looks finished.
15. **Second revelation & decision (obsessive drive):** cornered, you're offered the **big final payout**
    — the escape your old self always wanted. You either grab the exit or double down, obsessed.
16. **Audience revelation:** you discover **what Cem actually did** — he quietly refused life-changing
    money for decades and seeded a **tenant collective** to protect the corner; his "stubbornness" you
    resented was *love*. The ghost recontextualized.
17. **Third revelation & decision:** **Frau Brandt's turn** — she hands you the one lever (a clause, a
    deadline, a vote) that makes a stand *possible*, and a final choice crystallizes.
18. **Gate / gauntlet / visit to death:** the **longest, coldest night** — alone in the Späti, snow on
    the glass, the sign flickering, one decision left. A near-closure.

**— THE TURN (climax) —**
19. **Battle:** the climax is the **decision and its playing-out**: sign the sale and go *vs.* stand with
    the Kiez (a Kiezversammlung / a public stand at the corner). *(The final decision-point + consequence montage.)*
20. **Self-revelation:** you realize **the place is its people, and belonging is chosen by taking
    responsibility** — and you make peace with Cem.
21. **Moral decision:** you **refuse the payout and stand** (positive) — *or* take the money and run
    (negative revelation). **The endings are these branches.**

**— SPRING (coda: new equilibrium) —**
22. **New equilibrium:** the year turns; see §6 endings — the Kiez holds, is mourned with dignity, is
    sold out from under, or barely held — the hero at a higher or lower level.

## 5. The moral argument as a 7-step values progression *(the theme, structurally)*
People feel powerless against money and change **(weakness)** → they want to belong and be cared for
**(desire)** → the developer, the chain, and plain indifference grind them down **(opposition)** →
small acts of care and standing together **(plan)** → the Späti's worth was never its margin but its
web of people **(self-revelation)** → choose community over the payout **(moral decision)** → a Kiez
that holds, or is grieved honestly **(new equilibrium).**

## 6. Endings (= the step-21 moral decision → step-22 new equilibrium)
Selected from `{kiezTrust, integrity, cash, key flags}` (ties the sim to the arc):
- **Institution** *(high Kiez-Trust + high Integrity + solvent)*: you stand; the collective holds the
  corner; the Späti is beloved and viable. The dream — you belong.
- **Held the Line** *(high Kiez-Trust, thin cash)*: you stay, scarred and broke but rooted; the Kiez
  intact, the future uncertain. Bittersweet.
- **Sold Out** *(low Kiez-Trust / low Integrity, high cash)*: you take the money and go — rich, free,
  hollow; Cem's corner becomes a show flat. The negative revelation.
- **Pushed Out** *(low cash, regardless)*: you couldn't make rent — but *how you're remembered* (warm
  farewell vs. forgotten) flips on Kiez-Trust. Loss with, or without, dignity.

## 6b. The ending tapestry — *the finale = main ending × every side arc*
The ending is **not one screen but a weave**: the **main ending** (§6) frames it; then a **"where are they
now" epilogue montage** plays for **every recurring character you met** — so the close is literally *"a
summary of the main story + all the side stories."*

- **Each recurring strand has a branching *local outcome*** — its 7th step resolves differently by the
  choices you made *in that arc* (e.g. Lena: *found her voice* / *sold out her art* / *gave up and left*).
  **Most customers are one-off** texture (§9); the **handful of recurring characters** — the
  ending-load-bearing ones — are where the multi-visit, branching arcs live.
- **The chosen main ending recolors every side epilogue.** Each character's final beat = a function of
  **{their local outcome} × {your main ending}**. The same well-resolved arc reads warm under *Institution*
  and bittersweet under *Sold Out* (the corner that held them is gone). Your one big decision reaches into
  every relationship.
- **Worked example — Lena:**
  | local outcome ↓ / main ending → | Institution | Held the Line | Sold Out | Pushed Out |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | **Found her voice** | her mural goes up *inside the Späti* | she paints you a sign for the door | her piece sells; she can't bear to visit the show-flat | she paints the boarded window — a memorial |
  | **Gave up / left** | your corner pulls her back to art | a postcard: still trying, somewhere | she never knew the place that might've saved her | the city took another one |
- **Why it lands:** engaging a side character (a) **advances its branching arc**, (b) **grows the
  community mechanics** (see GDD §4b — the game *becomes* more about the Kiez), and (c) **feeds Kiez-Trust**,
  which **steers the main ending** — which then **recolors that very character's epilogue**. Engagement is
  the single through-line from "transactional shopkeeper" to "the heart of the corner."
- **Data model:** each strand carries `localOutcome` (from its branch flags) + `epilogue:
  Record<MainEnding, Record<LocalOutcome, line>>`. The finale composes `mainEndingNarration + Σ
  strand.epilogue[mainEnding][localOutcome]` over **only the characters you met** — the montage is *your* Kiez.

## 7. Symbol web
**The neon sign** (the place's heartbeat — flickering in crisis, steady at the end) · **Cem's keys** ·
**the hand-written prices** (care vs. algorithm) · **the bell over the door** · **the Pfand crate**
(dignity / what you owe the people who have least) · **the bench out front** (the Kiez gathering).

## 8. Side-story strands — each a complete **7-step** arc
*(The "curiosity" stories: Berlin's diversity, quirks, and history walking through the door. Each is
**data on the vignette registry**, advanced across multiple visits, gated by day/Kiez-Trust/flags. The
seven steps = Weakness&Need · Desire · Opponent · Plan · Battle · Self-revelation · New equilibrium.)*

### S1 — Lena, the broke art student *(the "extend credit?" thread)*
1. **W&N:** proud to a fault; would rather go hungry than admit she's broke (psych); cadges and dodges
   paying, straining the small trust of the corner (moral). 2. **Desire:** finish the piece for her first
   real show without selling out. 3. **Opponent:** her own pride + a slick gallerist who'll show her only
   if she makes "marketable" work. 4. **Plan:** scrape by on Mate and your goodwill; cut the corner.
   5. **Battle:** the show — does she hang the honest piece or the sellable one. 6. **Self-rev:** accepting
   help isn't failure; honesty is the work. 7. **New eq:** she pays off her tab with a small painting for
   the Späti wall — and becomes a regular who *gives*.

### S2 — Bottle-Jürgen, the Flaschensammler *(dignity & the Pfand)*
1. **W&N:** invisible, ashamed of how he gets by (psych); has stopped letting anyone *see* him, refusing
   connection (moral — he hurts those who'd care for him by vanishing). 2. **Desire:** get through the
   winter; keep his dignity. 3. **Opponent:** the cold, the new chain that locks its bins, a city that
   looks through him. 4. **Plan:** the bottle route, the quiet rhythm; your crate left out for him.
   5. **Battle:** a freezing night he doesn't show. 6. **Self-rev:** he is *seen* here; he matters to this
   corner. 7. **New eq:** he becomes the Späti's unofficial night-watch and weather-teller — Berlin's
   bottle-collector culture, rendered with grace.

### S3 — Mehmet, the döner neighbour *(ally + his own fight; Cem's oldest friend)*
1. **W&N:** carries the Kiez's memory but is bitter, resigned that everything good gets sold (psych);
   pushes away his estranged son who "left like you did" (moral). 2. **Desire:** keep his shop and pass it
   to a son who won't take it. 3. **Opponent:** the same developer; time; his own stubbornness. 4. **Plan:**
   stand with you; reach out, clumsily, to his son. 5. **Battle:** his shop's lease renewal / the son's
   visit. 6. **Self-rev:** legacy isn't the shop, it's the people. 7. **New eq:** reconciles (or doesn't)
   with his son; either way, fully your ally. *(Mirrors the main arc's father/uncle wound.)*

### S4 — Aylin & Jonas, the 6am techno couple *(Berlin club culture)*
1. **W&N:** they live for the next weekend, terrified of the ordinary life creeping up (psych); drifting
   from each other behind the euphoria (moral — careless with each other). 2. **Desire:** keep the magic
   alive. 3. **Opponent:** time, rent, the comedown, growing up. 4. **Plan:** one more season of Sundays
   glowing on your bench. 5. **Battle:** a pregnancy scare / a job offer in another city. 6. **Self-rev:**
   the magic was each other, not the club. 7. **New eq:** they keep coming — at 8pm now, not 8am. *(Berghain-
   dawn warmth without naming it.)*

### S5 — Frau Schulz, the East-Berlin Oma *(GDR history / Ostalgie)*
1. **W&N:** lonely; lives in the past (psych); sharp-tongued, pushes away the "Wessis" and the young who
   "don't know" (moral). 2. **Desire:** to not be the last one who remembers this corner *before*.
   3. **Opponent:** forgetting; the glossy new Kiez that erases her city. 4. **Plan:** hold court at the
   counter, tell the stories whether you listen or not. 5. **Battle:** the day they pull down a landmark she
   loved. 6. **Self-rev:** the young *do* want the stories — they're hers to give, not hoard. 7. **New eq:**
   she becomes the Späti's living history; you write her prices in her old GDR hand. *(She watched the Wall
   fall from this very corner — a quiet thread of real history.)*

### S6 — Karim, the new arrival *(Berlin's migration story)*
1. **W&N:** unmoored, ashamed of his halting German (psych); keeps everyone at arm's length so no one sees
   his fear (moral). 2. **Desire:** to belong somewhere in this enormous cold city. 3. **Opponent:**
   bureaucracy, the language, his own isolation. 4. **Plan:** small nightly exchanges at your counter become
   his German class and his anchor. 5. **Battle:** an Ausländerbehörde deadline / a racist incident at the
   corner. 6. **Self-rev:** this corner is already home; he has people. 7. **New eq:** he brings his cooking
   to a Kiez street party; the Späti helped a stranger become a neighbour.

### S7 — Achim, the old punk *(80s squat-scene Berlin)*
1. **W&N:** the last of his crew; clings to a dead scene (psych); scorns everyone who "sold out," which is
   now everyone (moral). 2. **Desire:** to matter again; to not be a fossil. 3. **Opponent:** time, the
   sanitized city, his own contempt. 4. **Plan:** haunt the corner, rail against the chain. 5. **Battle:**
   the building that was his squat is converted to lofts. 6. **Self-rev:** the fight continues in small
   acts — your stand *is* the scene, reborn. 7. **New eq:** he becomes the Kiez's elder agitator, useful at
   last in the campaign to save the corner. *(Kreuzberg/Friedrichshain squat history.)*

### S8 — Tobias, the transplant *(the fake-ally-opponent, humanized)*
1. **W&N:** buys belonging because he doesn't know how to earn it (psych); his money reshapes the place he
   claims to love (moral). 2. **Desire:** to be a real local, not a tourist with a lease. 3. **Opponent:**
   the Kiez's (justified) suspicion; his own obliviousness. 4. **Plan:** tip big, "support local," try to
   befriend you. 5. **Battle:** the moment he must choose between his startup's interests (allied with the
   developer) and the corner. 6. **Self-rev:** belonging is paid in care, not euros. 7. **New eq:** he either
   genuinely throws in with the Kiez — or moves on to the next "authentic" neighbourhood, a small tragedy.

### S9 — Nadia, the night-shift nurse & single mum *(Berlin's working night)*
1. **W&N:** running on empty, never asks for anything (psych); too proud/tired to lean on the community that
   would hold her (moral). 2. **Desire:** keep her kid fed and her head above water. 3. **Opponent:** the
   shifts, the money, the exhaustion. 4. **Plan:** the 4am coffee-and-Schrippe ritual at your counter.
   5. **Battle:** a night her childcare falls through mid-shift. 6. **Self-rev:** it takes a Kiez; asking
   isn't weakness. 7. **New eq:** the corner quietly minds her kid sometimes; she's part of the web.

### S10 — Robin, the kid from the provinces *(Berlin as refuge / chosen family)*
1. **W&N:** fled a small town that had no room for who they are; braced for rejection (psych); tests people
   coldly before trusting (moral). 2. **Desire:** to be fully themselves and find their people. 3. **Opponent:**
   old shame; a city that can be lonely as easily as freeing. 4. **Plan:** orbit the late-night corner, the
   safest-feeling light on the street. 5. **Battle:** a hard call home / a first heartbreak. 6. **Self-rev:**
   chosen family is real and it's *here*. 7. **New eq:** brings their new crew to the Späti — the corner as
   a place Berlin lets you become yourself.

### S11 — Frau Brandt, the developer's agent *(the fake-opponent-ally; ties to the main arc)*
1. **W&N:** believes change is inevitable so caring is pointless (psych); does harm efficiently and sleeps
   fine (moral). 2. **Desire:** close the deal, make partner. 3. **Opponent:** you, the Kiez — and her own
   buried memory of an erased childhood Kiez. 4. **Plan:** charm, pressure, the generous number. 5. **Battle:**
   the moment she can push the deal through *or* hand you the lever to stop it. 6. **Self-rev:** she's been
   erasing her own childhood, over and over. 7. **New eq:** she defects just enough — or doesn't, and lives
   with it. *(Her arc gates the main-arc step 17.)*

### S12 — Esra & Nilay, grandmother & granddaughter *(migration, generations)*
1. **W&N:** Esra clings to the old ways, fears being forgotten (psych); shames Nilay for "becoming German,"
   pushing her away (moral). 2. **Desire:** Esra — to be remembered; Nilay — to be herself without losing her
   grandmother. 3. **Opponent:** each other's pride; assimilation's pull. 4. **Plan:** they meet on neutral
   ground — your counter — over the foods Esra still buys. 5. **Battle:** a blow-up over Nilay's choices (a
   partner, a move). 6. **Self-rev:** love isn't sameness. 7. **New eq (branches):** *reconciled / fragile
   truce / estranged.* *(German-Turkish Kiez heart.)*

### S13 — Herr Bley, the lonely Wessi banker *(the 3am confessional)*
1. **W&N:** armored in money + status (psych); treats everyone — you included — as service (moral).
   2. **Desire:** to be *known* by someone who wants nothing from him. 3. **Opponent:** his contempt for "this
   part of town"; loneliness. 4. **Plan:** the nightly whisky-and-water; slowly, he talks. 5. **Battle:** a
   crisis (a divorce, a collapse) strips the armor. 6. **Self-rev:** the corner saw him when his world didn't.
   7. **New eq (branches):** *a quiet patron/ally / relapses into the armor / leaves a surprising gift.*
   *(Class + loneliness across Berlin's lines.)*

### S14 — Diego & Frau Pohl, the busker & the noise complaint *(you as mediator)*
1. **W&N:** Diego needs to be heard, literally (psych), and plays on regardless of who's trying to sleep
   (moral); Frau Pohl, sleepless and unheard, weaponizes complaints. 2. **Desire:** Diego — to make it; Pohl —
   peace. 3. **Opponent:** each other. 4. **Plan:** you're the neutral ground both trust. 5. **Battle:** the
   police/eviction-threat night. 6. **Self-rev:** each learns the other is a person. 7. **New eq (branches):**
   *a truce + a Späti-step gig / Diego moves on / Pohl gets her quiet, lonelier.* *(A community-mechanic hook.)*

### S15 — Bruno, the dog whose owner stopped coming *(quiet grief)*
1. **W&N (the Kiez's):** old Herr Voss, a regular, stops showing; his dog Bruno waits outside; the corner
   avoids the obvious (psych); no one wants to be the one who asks (moral). 2. **Desire:** to know what
   happened; to do right by Bruno. 3. **Opponent:** everyone's reluctance to face it. 4. **Plan:** you feed
   Bruno, ask around. 5. **Battle:** the truth — Voss has died / gone into care. 6. **Self-rev:** a corner
   grieves its own. 7. **New eq (branches):** *you adopt Bruno (he becomes the Späti's dog) / find him a home
   in the Kiez / he's taken away.* *(Mortality, gently — the Späti as the neighbourhood's memory.)*

> **Registry stays open** for one-offs that can *graduate* into strands (the influencer who keeps
> "discovering" you; a Wendekind reckoning with two Berlins; a runaway you might shelter). A new strand = data:
> its 7 steps + a **branching local outcome** + **per-main-ending epilogue lines** (§6b).

## 9. One-off "curiosity" vignette bank *(texture, not full arcs)*
A written pool of short, atmospheric interactions (no 7-step obligation; some recur and *graduate* into
strands). Samples: the tourist who can't find Berghain (and shouldn't); the drunk philosopher; the 5am
proposal; the lost wedding guest; the film crew that wants to rent your corner; the pigeon situation; the
power-cut sing-along; the "is this the real Berlin?" influencer. **~25–35 at MVP**, drawn by night/season/
weather — they make the city feel *inexhaustible* between the structured strands.

## 10. MVP narrative scope & delivery in play
- **Ship:** the **complete main 22-step arc** (it *is* the season); **~14 recurring side strands** each a
  full **branching 7-step** arc with a **local outcome** + **per-main-ending epilogue lines** (S1–S15; S11
  ties the main arc); the **ending tapestry** (§6b); and the **~25–35 one-off** curiosity pool. **Most
  customers are one-off** texture — the recurring strands are the few, branching, ending-connected ones.
- **Delivery:** the **vignette/quest registry** (`engine/content/vignettes.ts` + `arcs.ts`) stores each
  strand as ordered, **branching** beats with `{trigger: season/day/kiezTrust/flags, lines,
  options→effects+nextFlag, branch→localOutcome}` + `epilogue[mainEnding][localOutcome]`; a beat fires when
  its character visits and its trigger is met (main-arc beats fire at season/decision points). **Acceptance:**
  every shipped strand plays to a 7-step completion *and* reaches each of its branch local outcomes; the main
  arc hits its mapped 22 beats; the **finale composes the main ending + the epilogues of every met character**
  (§6b); all resolve from `{kiezTrust, integrity, cash, flags}`.
- **Writing first:** per the content-depth playbook, build the registry + a **writing style guide** (warm,
  funny, Berlin-specific without caricature; German flavour — Kiez, Pfand, Feierabend, Späti — over an
  English base) **before** scaling the prose. The writing *is* the product.
