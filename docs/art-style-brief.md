# Art style brief — pasted into every Codex art prompt

**Target:** a Berlin *Späti* (late-night corner kiosk) version of **Coffee Talk** — HD, hand-drawn-looking
**pixel art**. The canonical reference is `concept-art/main character target state.png` (the protagonist at a
counter): that image *is* the bar for rendering quality, pixel density, palette and mood. Match it.

**Rendering:** crisp pixel art with a consistent pixel grid (every "pixel" the same size — no mixed grids,
no soft anti-aliased edges, no smooth gradients, no vector look, no 3D render look, no photo texture).
Rich, painterly cluster shading with 4–6 tones per material, deliberate dithering only where Coffee Talk
uses it (soft light falloff), clean 1-px dark outlines on characters and props, warm rim light on faces
and hair. It should read as a skilled pixel artist's work, **not** blocky 8-bit/16-bit retro and **not**
"pixelated photo".

**Palette / light:** deep warm browns and amber lamp light inside vs. cool blue night and rain outside;
neon magenta + cyan accents (signs, fridge glow); soft grain. Bittersweet, cozy, a little melancholy.
Night-time always.

**Framing:** fixed side-on view from behind the counter (the player is the shopkeeper). Characters are
head-and-shoulders busts, centred, facing the viewer at the counter, slightly below eye level, like Coffee
Talk's customers. Nothing is drawn from above or in isometric.

**Berlin re-skin (content):** generic stand-in products only — never real brands: a green "Sterni"-like
beer bottle with a plain paper label, a yellow-label "Mate"-like bottle, unbranded chips/chocolate/gum,
tobacco pouches. Berlin texture: a U-Bahn-style sign, a techno flyer, a football scarf (no real club),
stickers on glass, plants, a Pfand (deposit) crate of empty bottles, a glowing drinks fridge wall, a hand-
painted "SPÄTI" sign. German flavour, no caricature.

**Character design language:** strong silhouette, one signature prop or garment per character, expressive
faces (Coffee Talk proportions: realistic-ish heads, big readable eyes, subtle mouths), everyday clothes with
one saturated accent colour. The protagonist ("You", Cem's nephew) is *exactly* the man in the target image:
mid-20s, messy wavy brown hair, light stubble, red hoodie under a brown/black jacket with a red band.

**Absolutely not:** text, watermarks, signatures, borders, frames, UI mockup chrome, multiple panels,
sprite-sheet grids, isometric/top-down views, chibi/anime-big-head proportions, glossy smooth shading,
real brands or logos, photographic backgrounds.
